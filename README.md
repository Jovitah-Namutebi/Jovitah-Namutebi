# Jovitah Namutebi

**PhD Student in Economics | Tufts University**

Welcome to my GitHub profile!

I am a PhD student in Economics at Tufts University. My research interests lie at the intersection of **development economics, political economy, and household economics**, with a particular focus on developing countries.

My research examines how institutions, family networks, and economic constraints shape household welfare, resource allocation, and economic opportunities.

## Research Interests

- Development Economics
- Political Economy and Decentralization
- Household Economics and Informal Insurance
- Labor Mobility and Migration
- Applied Microeconomics

## Research in Progress

**Family Networks and Informal Insurance**

Examining how extended family networks respond to individual economic shocks within married households, and the implications for informal insurance and intra-household resource allocation.

**Decentralization and Local Economic Development**

Studying the economic consequences of administrative decentralization, with a focus on local economic activity and development outcomes.

**Labor Mobility and Migration**

Exploring barriers to labor mobility and their implications for access to employment opportunities in developing countries.
